<Chapter n="i." label="Problem">

A team can create 3.1 expected goals, dominate a match, and lose 1–0 to a breakaway. Tables built on results reward luck along with quality. The **Expected Result** asks what *should* have happened, based on the rate at which each side creates and concedes chances.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="ii." label="Process">

Every team gets an attack rating and a defence rating as multipliers of the league average. Those produce expected goal rates for each fixture, which go through a Dixon-Coles bivariate Poisson to give a full scoreline grid, and from there win, draw and loss probabilities and expected points.

- **Time-decayed.** Every match is weighted by how recent it is, so form matters without an arbitrary "last 10 games" cut-off.
- **Shrunk.** Gamma priors stop three good results from producing an absurd rating in August.
- **Cross-season.** Last season's ratings carry over, regressed 30% toward the mean. Promoted clubs start from a fitted baseline.
- **xG-blended.** Ratings fitted on goals and on shot quality, blended at 65% weight on xG.
- **Dixon-Coles corrected.** Fixes plain Poisson's mispricing of low scores like 0–0 and 1–1.
- **Rebuilt hourly** by GitHub Actions, for five leagues fitted independently.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="iii." label="Result">

The first version scored **worse than the league base rate**: you would have done better predicting the same three numbers for every match. That's what prompted the rebuild.

| Model | RPS ↓ | Log loss ↓ | Accuracy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **xR (current)** | **0.2069** | **1.0281** | **49.4%** |
| League base rate | 0.2271 | 1.0843 | 42.0% |
| xR (previous) | 0.2326 | 1.1164 | 40.9% |

*Walk-forward backtest on 350 matches of the 2025–26 Premier League. Every forecast uses only matches played before kick-off.*

The rebuild improved RPS by 11%. These numbers are written by the backtest script, not typed by hand, because a hand-maintained table drifted from the code twice.

</Chapter>
